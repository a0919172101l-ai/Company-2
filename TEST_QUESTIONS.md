13. What is the company's business model?
14. How does the company generate revenue?
15. What payment methods does the company propose?
16. Does the company offer monthly subscriptions?
17. Does the company offer annual licenses?
18. What training courses does the company provide?
19. What facilities or equipment does the company need?
20. What is the company's investment plan?
21. How will investment money be used?
22. What are the main business risks?
23. How will the company reduce those risks?
24. What information is still missing from the company documents?
